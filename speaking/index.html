Keynote Address · University Career Panel

2026 Freshers’ Orientation & Career Panel: “Beyond the Classroom”

Institution: Fountain University, Osogbo • Date: 3 November 2026 • Engagement: Keynote Speaker · Alumnus
Read University Coverage →

Invited as distinguished alumnus, CITN Fellow, and finance professional to deliver the keynote address to incoming students at the Campus Auditorium in Osogbo. The address challenged students to move beyond passive reliance on academic paper qualifications alone, focusing on developing practical market-relevant skills, building enduring professional relationships, pursuing entrepreneurial opportunities, and preparing for the changing demands of the modern labour market.

Official Publication: “FUO Alumni Task Freshers on Skills, Career Development” (Fountain University)
Coverage: Official FUO Portal · Campus Auditorium, Osogbo
Keynote Address · Civic & Economic Leadership

“Beyond Survival: The Economics of Value Creation, Capital Retention, and Community Resilience”

Institution: Rotary Club of Ikeja Golden Impact • Date: 23 October 2026 • Engagement: Guest Keynote Speaker
Rotary District 9110 · Ikeja, Lagos

Invited as guest speaker before district leaders, Rotarians, and commercial executives in Ikeja to deliver an economic and institutional keynote address under the meeting theme: “Building Sustainable Communities Through Entrepreneurship, Skills Acquisition, and Economic Empowerment.” The address examined the civilizational paradox of modern psychological fragility, challenged middle-class capital flight by contrasting domestic migration outflows with foreign risk capital building local supply chains, and called on business leaders to transition from tactical survival mode to compounding, generational value creation in service of community resilience.

Theme & Ethical Alignment: Entrepreneurship, Technical Skills Acquisition · Rotary's Service Above Self & The 4-Way Test
District 9110 Assembly · Ikeja, Lagos
