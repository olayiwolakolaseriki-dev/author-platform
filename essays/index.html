Essay • 8 min read • Theme: Ambition & Power

The Myth of the Neutral Ambition

We often speak of ambition as though it were pure, sterile energy—a neutral drive toward excellence. But ambition is never empty; it always takes the shape of the vessel into which it is poured. In post-colonial Africa, that vessel has almost uniformly been manufactured abroad.

When we praise a young professional for "making it," what metric are we truly celebrating? Are we applauding institutional stewardship, or are we simply validating their ability to pass through imperial sieve-gates with immaculate compliance?

Essay • 11 min read • Theme: Cultural Conditioning

Inheriting Scripts: Why Success Still Looks Foreign

Language is the first border we cross. For many educated Africans, the language of our parents’ intimacy is kept strictly separate from the language of our intellect. We have been trained to believe that serious thought, strategic leadership, and civilizational advancement must occur in an adopted syntax.

This essay explores the subtle psychological dissociation that results when prestige is fundamentally alienated from home.
